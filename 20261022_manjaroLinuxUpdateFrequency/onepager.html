Verdict

  • Stable is paused, not slower. Before the pause, the rhythm did not slow down: 15 d median in 2026, 13 d overall.
  • Freeze until the GitLab → Forgejo move is done (topic 189985).
  • GitLab was slow and broke kernel builds (topic 189954).
  • Maintainer: "super limited time" (topic 189985).
  • Next update will be large: Plasma 6.8, systemd 262, kernel 7.2/7.3.
  • Action: linux71 is end of life since 7.1.13 (topic 190447). Move to linux72 or keep linux618 (LTS).

Measure it yourself

  1. Read the stable announcements: forum.manjaro.org/c/announcements/stable-updates/12.rss
  2. Check the date of the last branch change: curl -s https://mirror.init7.net/manjaro/stable/state
  3. Compare branch versions: ./measure_branch_lag.sh
  4. Count your own update waves: ./measure_local_waves.sh
  5. Rebuild this page: python3 make_onepager.py
The state date also changes for small security fixes. It is not proof of a full update.
