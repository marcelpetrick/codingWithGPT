Task: a polite, clear decline of an unsolicited offer.
- Thank the sender briefly and acknowledge the specific offer in a few words.
- Say clearly that there is no need or interest at the moment. Do not give a reason that invites a counter-argument.
- Do not say "I am not the right person". Do not ask for material, a call or a follow-up.
- Stay friendly. No sarcasm, no robotic wording.
